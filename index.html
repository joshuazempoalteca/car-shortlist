04 / THE DECISION
01 / NEW SELECT SPORT

Keep the starting price down.

At your prices, the new Select Sport costs $2,000 less than the used Preferred and $1,000 less than the Civic. It fits a buyer who values a known history and new-car coverage.

Before committingGet a dealer’s written out-the-door quote; the builder currently shows $26,855 rather than your $26,000 target.

02 / CERTIFIED PREFERRED

Trade a few miles for more comfort.

This Preferred adds heated front seats, a power/memory driver’s seat and a moonroof. At 9,177 miles and a $28,000 comparison price, it is a comfort-and-coverage choice rather than an AWD upgrade.

Before committingConfirm Mazda CPO enrollment, original in-service date, service history and a written out-the-door price.

03 / CIVIC HYBRID

Make more miles cost less.

Its 49-mpg combined rating lowers fuel spending, but this specific car has 40,310 miles—31,133 more than the used Mazda. Weigh the fuel savings against condition and remaining coverage.

Before committingResolve the Sport/Touring description conflict, review history and hybrid condition, and price shipping and insurance.
